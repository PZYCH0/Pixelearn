Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

Interactive Worksheet

Rules

Comparative = compare two things. tall → taller
Superlative = compare three or more. tall → tallest
  • Short adjectives: add -er / -est
  • Ends in -y: change y → i (happy → happier → happiest)
  • Long adjectives: use more / most (more interesting)
  • Irregular: good → better → best, bad → worse → worst

Exercise 1: Complete the table

Adjective Comparative Superlative
tall
small
big
happy
good
interesting
Answer key (Exercise 1): tall → taller / tallest; small → smaller / smallest; big → bigger / biggest; happy → happier / happiest; good → better / best; interesting → more interesting / most interesting.

Exercise 2: Choose the correct answer

1) My house is (bigger / biggest) than yours.

2) This exercise is the (easier / easiest) in the book.

3) A car is (faster / fastest) than a bicycle.

4) English is (more interesting / most interesting) than history for me.

5) Today is the (hotter / hottest) day of the week.

Answer key (Exercise 2): 1 bigger, 2 easiest, 3 faster, 4 more interesting, 5 hottest.

Exercise 3: Complete the sentences

1) My brother is (tall) than me.

2) This is the (good) movie I have ever seen.

3) A lion is (dangerous) than a dog.

4) This is the (bad) test of the year.

5) Summer is (hot) than spring.

Answer key (Exercise 3): 1 taller, 2 best, 3 more dangerous, 4 worst, 5 hotter.

Exercise 4: Rewrite the sentences

1) My bag is heavy. Your bag is light. (lighter)

2) Math is difficult. English is easy. (easier)

3) This book is interesting. That book is very interesting. (most)

Sample answers (Exercise 4):
1) Your bag is lighter than my bag. / Your bag is lighter than mine.
2) English is easier than math.
3) That book is the most interesting (book).

Exercise 5: Write your own sentences

These two are open answers (no automatic marking).
