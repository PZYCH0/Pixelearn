Lesson: In My Classroom

Unit 3: Grammar & Communication
Class: 2-1 | Date: 2025-11-19

What We Will Do Today

1. Warm-up (5 minutes)

Activity: Look at pictures of Nila's floating school.

What to do: Answer questions about Nila and her school. Then, talk about what is the same in your school.

2. Presentation (12 minutes)

Classroom Objects Vocabulary (6 min)

Activity: Look at flashcards or real objects: book, pen, pencil, marker, backpack, eraser, board, desk, notebook, ruler, computer, calculator, pencil sharpener, paper.

What to do: Listen to the teacher say the words. Repeat after the teacher. Point to objects when asked.

Demonstratives Grammar (6 min)

Activity: Learn sentences like: "This is my pen." (near) "That is my backpack." (far) "These are my books." (near/plural) "Those are my markers." (far/plural)

What to do: Listen and repeat the sentences. Notice the difference between this/that (near/far) and these/those (plural).

3. Practice (20 minutes)

A. Chant (4 min)

What to do: Listen to the chant "Is this your book?" Repeat it. Pick up items and chant with a partner.

B. Label the Classroom (6 min)

What to do: Look at the worksheet. Label the numbers with classroom objects. Check your answers with a partner or listen to audio.

C. Pair Practice (10 min)

What to do: Put 2-3 small objects on your desk. Practice with your partner: "Is this your pen?" "Yes, that's my pen." / "No, that's not my pen."

4. Production (20 minutes)

Activity: Have real conversations using classroom objects.

What to do: Use objects around the classroom. Ask and answer questions like "Is this your pen?" or "Are these your books?"
