Lesson 6: Listen & Practice Speaking

Level: A1 | Duration: 45 min | Theme: Describing People

Lesson Objective

In this lesson, students will learn how to describe people in their family using adjectives like tall, short, young, old, thin, big.

Part A: Listen & Repeat

1. Look at the photo and listen. Then listen and repeat.

He is big. She is young. He is thin.
She is old. He is tall. He is short.

Part B: Practice the Dialogue

Cyndy: This is my brother.
Hicham: He is tall!
Cyndy: Yes, he is.
Hicham: Do you have a sister?
Cyndy: Yes, I do.
Hicham: Is she tall?
Cyndy: No, she isn’t. She’s short.

Practice this dialogue with a partner. Switch roles.

Part C: Vocabulary Practice

Listen and point to the words you hear, then repeat.

short • tall • big • thin • old • young

Part D: Listening Task

1. Circle the words you hear.
2. Now listen again and choose the correct picture.

Words: big, young, short, old, thin, tall

Part E: Speaking Practice

Example:
A: Is your brother young or old?
B: Yes, he is.
A: Is he big or thin?
B: No, he isn’t. He’s thin.
A: Is he tall or short?
B: Yes, he is short.

Part F: Draw and Talk

Draw a picture of a person and describe them to your partner:

This is my _____________________.
He / She is _____________________.
He / She is _____________________.

Language Box

Is he / she…?
Yes, he is. / No, she isn’t.
