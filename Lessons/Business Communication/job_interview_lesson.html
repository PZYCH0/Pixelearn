Job Interview English Practice

A2–B1 | 60–75 min | Online Lesson

Lesson Objectives

  • Answer 5 common job interview questions in simple English.
  • Use polite and structured responses for interviews.
  • Practice real-time speaking with a partner.

Watch & Learn

Watch this video and write down any interview questions you hear:

Key Vocabulary

Match the words with their meanings: Describe yourself, Strengths, Weaknesses, Motivated, Achievement, Goals.

Practice Questions

Common Interview Questions:

  • Tell us about yourself.
  • Why do you want this job?
  • Why should we hire you?
  • Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
  • What motivates you?

Role-Play: Mock Interview

Work in pairs: One is the interviewer, one is the interviewee. Use at least 3 questions. Switch roles after 5 minutes.
Download Cheat Sheet

Hot Seat Game

One student sits in the “hot seat.” Rapid-fire answer 5 questions (mix interview + fun ones like “What’s your favorite food?”).

Homework

Write and record yourself answering 5 interview questions. Practice pronunciation and timing.
